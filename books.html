Gods of the Clay Tablets

The Anunnaki, the Emerald Tablets, and the Lost History of Civilization

An evidence-first reading of the oldest written stories: what the tablets actually say, what later ages added, and how to tell the difference.

Inside the book: four parts, from the gods who came down and the Tablet of Destinies, through the Emerald Tablets and Newton's secret translation work, to the modern mystery industry and the scholars' response, ending with a reader's field guide.

The Ritual Life

Ceremonial Magic from Solomon to Crowley and What It Means for Your Everyday Mind

A serious history of ceremonial magic, and a plain look at the habits of mind that ritual builds, for readers who practice and readers who are only curious.

Inside the book: the old grimoires and Agrippa, the Golden Dawn and Crowley's Cairo working, the architecture of a ritual and the Enochian engine, and a closing part on why ritual works in an ordinary morning.

The Descent of the Watchers
