The audiobook studio

Your book, narrated in your own voice.

OwnVoice Studio is the audiobook studio of Onikan Global Solutions LLC. It turns a finished manuscript into a finished audiobook narrated in the author’s own cloned voice. You record your voice, you give consent in the open, you review the narration, and the finished masters are yours outright.

Most authors face a hard choice. Record the audiobook yourself and spend days in front of a microphone, or hire a studio narrator at $2,000 to $5,000 and let a stranger carry your book. OwnVoice Studio is the third way: your voice, produced by the studio, at a fixed price confirmed in writing before production begins.

The narration is digitally produced from your own voice, and we say so plainly, on this page and on every master we deliver. Honesty about the method is part of the product.

The studio’s own home is ownvoice.tech, with the full process, the full pricing, and the studio’s terms.

How it works

From manuscript to masters, in five steps

  1. 1

    Share your manuscript. Tell us about your book and send the finished manuscript. We confirm your quote in writing before anything is produced.

  2. 2

    Record your voice. You record in your own voice and tick the consent attestations yourself. Your cloned voice is built from that recording, for your book.

  3. 3

    Approve the sound. We produce the narration and run the pronunciation review loop with you, checking names, places, and terms against your intent.

  4. 4

    Review your chapters. You listen and flag anything that needs another pass. We correct it and produce the finished masters.

  5. 5

    Own your masters. The finished masters are delivered to you and owned outright by you, to sell and distribute as you choose.

Consent and ethics

Your voice is yours. We work on that basis, in writing.

Voice cloning asks for trust, so the rules are stated before any recording happens, not after.

  • Only your own voice. We clone the voice of the author, recorded by the author. We do not clone the voice of any other person, and we do not build a voice from recordings you do not have the right to use.
  • Consent you give yourself. Intake includes consent attestations that you read and tick yourself, in the studio flow, before your voice is built. No consent, no clone. That is the rule, without exception.
  • Built for your book. Your cloned voice is built for your project and used for your project. It is not sold, not shared, and not used to narrate another author’s book. Service clones are deleted after delivery.
  • Disclosed, always. The finished audiobook is narrated by a digital clone of your voice, produced with your consent. We disclose that plainly, and we encourage you to disclose it wherever your audiobook is offered.

Straight answers

Questions, answered plainly

Who owns the finished audiobook?

You do. The finished masters are owned outright by you, the author. We produce them; you own them.

Whose voice narrates my book?

Yours, if you choose the own-voice route. You record in your own voice, confirm your consent, and we build a clone of your voice for your book. Ghost Narrator also offers a professional studio voice. Either way, the narration is digitally produced and disclosed as such.

Where can I sell it?

Spotify and direct sales welcome disclosed digital narration, and we will show you exactly how to place your book there. ACX bars AI narration, and we will never pretend otherwise.

What do you need from me to start?

A finished manuscript, a recording of your own voice, and your consent, given by you in the intake flow. Start by writing to contact@onikanglobal.com with your book title and its approximate length.
