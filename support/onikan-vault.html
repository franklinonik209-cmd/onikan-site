Onikan Vault Support

Onikan Vault is a private password manager from Onikan Global Solutions LLC. Your vault lives in a single encrypted file on your iPhone. There is no Onikan account and no Onikan server holding your passwords, so most help starts with the device in your hand.

Contact us

Email: contact@onikanglobal.com

Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and a plain description of what happened. Never send your master password, your recovery key, your vault file, or any saved password, card number, or other secret. Support cannot use them and does not need them.

If you forgot your master password

Onikan cannot reset your master password, and we cannot recover your vault for you. That limit is deliberate: nobody at Onikan can open your vault.

At setup, the app showed you a recovery key once inside a paper emergency kit. That key can open the existing vault on your device. Print the kit or copy the key onto paper by hand, and keep it with your important documents.

If both the master password and the recovery key are lost, the saved records cannot be recreated. The recovery key opens an existing vault file; it cannot rebuild your items if the iPhone or the vault file is lost.

Managing Onikan Vault Pro

Onikan Vault is free to use for up to 25 saved items, and every safety feature stays free. Optional Pro unlocks unlimited saved items.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your Apple ID account settings. To avoid a renewal charge, turn off auto-renewal at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period. Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase and does not renew.

Deleting your information

Your records stay on your device until you change them. Move a record to Trash to set it aside, restore it if you change your mind, or permanently delete it from Trash. Deleting the app from your device deletes the app container and the local vault file stored in it.

Privacy

Onikan Vault does not collect data from the app and does not track you. The full policy is at onikanglobal.com/privacy/onikan-vault.
