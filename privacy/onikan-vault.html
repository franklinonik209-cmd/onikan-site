Privacy Policy for Onikan Vault

Metropolitan Museum of Art, Open Access. License: CC0.

Onikan Vault is provided by Onikan Global Solutions LLC. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Onikan Vault app.

The short version is simple: Onikan Vault has no user account, no Onikan server for your vault, and no feature that sends your saved secrets to Onikan. Your vault is stored in an encrypted file on your device.

For Apple's App Privacy summary, the position is the same: Onikan Vault does not collect data from the app, and it does not track you.

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Information stored on your device

When you use Onikan Vault, the app stores the following information locally so the vault can work:

  • The records you choose to save, such as login usernames and passwords, website addresses, payment card details, identity details, secure notes, and custom fields.
  • Vault settings, such as the vault name and your auto-lock choice.
  • Technical information needed to protect and open the vault file, including encryption parameters, salts, wrapped encryption keys, ciphertext, creation and update times, a count of consecutive failed unlock attempts, and any active unlock wait deadline.

Your record titles, usernames, and secret values are kept inside the encrypted part of the vault file. They are not placed in the file header as plain text.

Onikan does not receive, view, or hold a copy of this local vault information.

What Onikan Vault does not collect

Onikan Vault does not:

  • Require or offer an Onikan account.
  • Send your vault contents to Onikan.
  • Operate a cloud service or sync service for your vault.
  • Include advertising.
  • Include analytics or crash reporting for Onikan.
  • Track you across apps or websites.
  • Sell or share your personal information.
  • Use your information for targeted advertising, profiling, or automated decisions about you.

The app contains no network code for sending vault data or usage data to Onikan or to an analytics provider.

How local information is used

Information in the vault is processed on your device only to:

  • Create and unlock the vault.
  • Show, search, add, edit, favorite, copy, move to trash, restore, and permanently delete your records.
  • Generate passwords at your request.
  • Apply your auto-lock setting and the unlock attempt limits.
  • Check whether Pro is active and apply the free item limit or the unlimited Pro item allowance.

Encryption and the recovery key

The vault uses AES-256-GCM encryption. Argon2id is used with your master password, and HKDF-SHA256 is used with the recovery key, to protect separate wrapped copies of the vault key.

Your master password is not saved in the vault file as plain text and is not sent to Onikan.

Your recovery key is held by you. At setup, the app shows the key once so you can print it or write it on paper as part of your emergency kit. The app does not show the key again, and Onikan does not know it. Anyone who has the recovery key and access to the vault file may be able to open the vault, so keep the paper kit private and secure.

Onikan cannot reset your master password, replace a lost recovery key, or recover vault contents for you.

Device backup and copying

The app marks its vault storage as excluded from iCloud backup. Onikan Vault version 0.1.0 does not provide cloud sync or a vault transfer feature.

When you choose to copy a value in the app, iOS places that value on the clipboard at your direction. Onikan does not receive copied values. Clear the clipboard after copying sensitive information, and review your Apple clipboard and Universal Clipboard settings if you use those features.

Purchases and subscriptions

Onikan Vault is free to download and use for up to 25 saved items. Optional Pro products unlock unlimited saved items. Safety features are not sold separately and are not paywalled.

The Pro products are:

  • Pro Monthly: an auto-renewable subscription.
  • Pro Yearly: an auto-renewable subscription.
  • Pro Lifetime: a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

Prices are shown by Apple in the app before you buy. The monthly and yearly subscriptions include a free trial for eligible subscribers; the exact trial duration is shown by Apple before purchase. The lifetime purchase has no trial.

Subscription terms:

  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase.
  • A subscription automatically renews unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period.
  • Your Apple ID account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current billing period, at the price shown by Apple.
  • You can manage a subscription or turn off auto-renewal at any time in your Apple ID account settings. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.
  • Any unused portion of a free trial period is forfeited when you purchase a subscription, where applicable.

If you buy a Pro product, Apple processes the purchase through your Apple ID. Onikan does not receive or store your payment card number or other payment credentials.

The app receives the entitlement information needed from Apple's StoreKit service to determine whether unlimited items should be unlocked on the device. Onikan does not use purchase information to track you, advertise to you, or build a profile. Apple's handling of App Store purchases is governed by Apple's privacy policy and your Apple account settings.

Terms of use

Use of Onikan Vault is governed by Apple's Standard End User License Agreement (EULA), available at https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/

Sharing

Onikan does not share vault information because Onikan does not receive it.

If you choose to make a purchase, Apple receives the information Apple needs to process that purchase. Onikan Vault does not provide your vault contents to Apple or to any advertiser, analytics company, data broker, or other third party.

Retention and deletion

Your vault information remains on your device until you change or delete it.

  • Editing a record replaces the saved values in the local encrypted vault.
  • Moving a record to Trash does not permanently erase it. A trashed record remains in the encrypted vault until you restore it, permanently delete it, or empty the Trash.
  • Deleting the app from your device deletes the app container and the local vault file stored in it, subject to normal iOS behavior.
  • Because Onikan does not hold a copy of your vault, Onikan cannot retrieve, correct, export, or delete your vault records for you.

If you contact Onikan outside the app, do not send your master password, recovery key, vault file, passwords, payment card numbers, or other secrets. Support cannot use them and does not need them.

Security

Onikan Vault uses modern published encryption methods, auto-lock, and escalating waits after repeated failed unlock attempts. No security method can remove every risk. You are responsible for using a strong master password, protecting the device, and keeping the paper emergency kit in a safe place.

If both the device or vault file and the paper emergency kit are lost, the saved records cannot be recreated from the recovery key alone.

Your privacy choices and state rights

Onikan does not sell or share personal information and does not use personal information for targeted advertising. Onikan does not use the sensitive information stored in your local vault to infer characteristics about you.

You control vault information directly in the app. You may view and correct records in the app, permanently delete records from Trash, or remove the local vault by deleting the app.

If you believe Onikan holds personal information about you outside the app, such as information you chose to send in a support message, you may contact us at the address below to ask about access, correction, or deletion. On request, we will delete that information unless we must keep it for a legal reason. We may need enough information to identify the separate record you mean.

Children

Onikan Vault is a general purpose security utility and is not designed for children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone because it does not collect personal information through the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this page with a new effective date. A future version of the app that changes how information is handled must be described in an updated policy before that handling begins.
