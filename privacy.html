Privacy policy

Metropolitan Museum of Art, Open Access. License: CC0.

Orbit is developed and published by Onikan Global Solutions LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy explains what Orbit does with information in the Android app.

Effective date: October 15, 2026

The short version

Orbit is a private, local-first journal. It has no account system, no Orbit server, no analytics, no advertising, and no cloud AI. Your journal is stored on your device. We do not collect, receive, sell, or share your journal entries or the people in them.

Information stored on your device

When you use Orbit, the app stores the journal content you create or import on your device. That local content can include:

  • Journal entry text
  • The date each entry is about
  • Optional place tags you add
  • People you create or confirm
  • Display names and aliases for people
  • Confirmed, suggested, or dismissed links between entries and people
  • Information needed for the app to show your timeline, person pages, graph, search results, and browse views

This information stays in the app’s local storage on your device unless you choose to export or restore a file as described below.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information from Orbit. The app does not ask you to create an account, does not include analytics or advertising software, and does not send your journal content to us or to a cloud service.

Orbit does not include a network API in this release. People detection runs on your device using inspectable rules, not a cloud AI service.

Files you import and export

Import and export are controlled by you.

  • If you choose a text, Markdown, Day One JSON, or Orbit backup file for import, Orbit reads the file you selected so it can add or restore the content in the app.
  • If you export a backup, Orbit creates a JSON file containing your journal so you can restore it later.
  • If you export the readable edition, Orbit creates a Markdown file containing your entries and a people index.
  • Exported files are not encrypted by Orbit. Treat a backup like a paper journal and store it in a place you trust.
  • Orbit does not upload an imported or exported file to us. Where you save, send, or back up an exported file is your choice and is governed by the service or storage you choose.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share your journal content with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers. Orbit shares information only when you take an action that moves a file you control, such as saving an export to a folder or another app.

Security

Your journal is protected by the security of your device and its operating system. Use a device lock and keep your device software up to date. Because exported backup files are plain files, anyone who can open an exported file can read it. Store backups carefully.

Retention and deletion

Your journal remains in the app’s local storage until you change it, delete entries, restore a backup over it, or uninstall the app.

Restoring a backup replaces the current journal in the app after confirmation. Uninstalling the app removes the app’s local storage from the device, but it does not delete backup or Markdown files that you previously saved somewhere else. You control those files and can delete them from the place where you stored them.

How deletion works in the app: an entry is deleted from that entry’s own screen (open the entry, tap Delete, confirm). There is no separate delete button for a person; a person is removed by merging them into another person (open the person’s page, open the menu, choose “Merge into…”), which deletes the merged record and moves its memories to the person who remains. Restoring a backup replaces the whole journal, and uninstalling the app removes its local storage.

Children

Orbit is a general-audience journaling app. It is not designed for or directed at children. The Play target audience declaration and content rating are completed in Play Console at submission.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how Orbit handles information, we will update this policy before that version is published. Any future sync, account, sharing, or network feature would require a policy update and clear in-app disclosure before use.
