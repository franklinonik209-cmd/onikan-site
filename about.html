About the house

Onikan Global Solutions LLC is a Pennsylvania limited liability company, formed in October 2026 and based in Taylor, Pennsylvania. It publishes books, produces media, and develops software.

Franklin Onikan, founder

Franklin Onikan is an author and publisher. His books, Gods of the Clay Tablets, The Ritual Life, and The Descent of the Watchers, read the ancient record on its own terms and leave the conclusions to the reader.

He hosts two YouTube channels built on the same rule: Tablets & Truth and The Forbidden Shelf. Show the evidence, plainly, and let people think for themselves.

The company is his publishing and media house: the books, the channels, and a growing shelf of software all live under the one mark, the Open Tablet.
