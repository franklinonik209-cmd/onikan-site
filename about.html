The house

A publishing and media house

Onikan Global is a publishing and media house built on a simple rule: put the primary record in front of people, plainly, and leave the conclusions to them.

The house publishes books, produces documentary media, and develops software from Taylor, Pennsylvania. Its catalogue begins with the ancient record: the clay tablets, the ritual texts, the watcher traditions, presented from the sources themselves, in editions a reader can check line by line.

Onikan Global Solutions LLC is a Pennsylvania limited liability company, formed in October 2026. It is the legal home of the books, the channels, and the software, all issued under the one mark.

Franklin Onikan

Founder & CEO

Franklin Onikan is an author and publisher. His books, Gods of the Clay Tablets, The Ritual Life, and The Descent of the Watchers, read the ancient record on its own terms and leave the conclusions to the reader. The catalogue also carries the Tablets & Truth Collection, and the audiobook edition of The Descent of the Watchers is complete, narrated by an AI rendering of the author's voice, and releases October 30, 2026.

He hosts two YouTube channels built on the same rule: Tablets & Truth and The Forbidden Shelf. Show the evidence, plainly, and let people think for themselves.

The company is his publishing and media house: the books, the channels, and a growing shelf of software live under the one mark, the Open Tablet. The house's first app, Orbit, is in review at Google Play, with a wider slate in development.
