ClearScan privacy policy

Metropolitan Museum of Art, Open Access. License: CC0.

ClearScan is made by Onikan Global Solutions LLC. Contact: contact@onikanglobal.com. This policy describes what the ClearScan app does with your information.

Effective November 5, 2026.

1. The short version

ClearScan is a camera and a filing cabinet. Your documents never leave your phone through ClearScan. There is no account, no cloud upload, no analytics, and no advertising. We collect nothing from you, because there is nowhere for anything to go: everything ClearScan stores lives in the app's private directory on your phone, and the only time a document leaves that directory is when you yourself choose to share it through your phone's own share sheet.

2. What ClearScan stores on your phone (and nowhere else)

When you scan, file, filter, sign, redact, or tag a document, ClearScan stores the following on your phone only:

  • The scanned page images, as image files in the app's private directory.
  • A library index entry for each document: its title, its folder (if you filed it in one), its tags, the text extracted from its pages by on-device OCR (so library search can find it), and its created and updated timestamps.
  • Filtered, signed, and redacted copies, when you make them: each is filed as a new document with its own page image. The original document is never modified.
  • If you set an app lock PIN: the PIN and a biometric on/off flag, in a file in the same private directory (see section 4 for the honest limit of this lock).
  • Exported PDFs and JPGs, only in the temporary directory ClearScan creates when you tap Share, and only until the share sheet takes over.

ClearScan does not store your documents, titles, tags, OCR text, or PIN on any server, because ClearScan has no server and no account system.

3. What ClearScan does not collect

  • No account information: there is no sign-in, no email address, no user ID, and no password anywhere in the product.
  • No location, no contacts, no calendar, no messages, no health data, and no financial information: the product has no feature that reads any of them.
  • No usage analytics, no crash reports, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking: no SDK for any of them is in the product.
  • No biometric data: the biometric prompt is your phone's own prompt. ClearScan receives only a pass or fail answer, and stores only your on/off choice, never a fingerprint or a face.
  • No payment data: see section 7.

4. The app lock, stated plainly

The app lock is a usability gate, not encryption. It keeps casual browsing out of the library; it does not encrypt anything. Your scanned pages are plain image files in the app's private directory, and the PIN itself is stored in plain text in a file in that same directory. A forgotten PIN has no recovery: removing it means clearing the app's data, which also removes the library. Do not treat the lock as protection against someone with full access to your unlocked phone or to its files. This section exists so the lock is never oversold.

5. Sharing: the one way a document leaves your phone

ClearScan itself never uploads, emails, or messages anything. The only way a document leaves your phone through ClearScan is when you tap Share (or the equivalent action) and then choose a destination in your phone's own share sheet, such as Files, Mail, or Messages. That destination receives the exported PDF because you chose it, under that destination's own privacy policy, not because ClearScan sent it. If you never tap Share, nothing leaves.

6. Camera, scanner, and OCR: processed on your phone

Scanning uses your phone maker's own scanner: Google ML Kit Document Scanner on Android, Apple VisionKit on iOS. Text extraction uses the matching on-device recognition: ML Kit Text Recognition on Android, Vision text recognition on iOS. Pages and extracted text stay in the app's private directory described in section 2. Neither the scanner nor the OCR step sends a page, an image, or extracted text to any server.

7. Purchases

No purchase can be made in this build. ClearScan's subscription products of record (ClearScan Pro, monthly and yearly) exist in code behind a dark switch, and the store billing library in the app stays inert while that switch is off: it makes no purchase, takes no payment, and sends us nothing. When subscriptions are activated in a future build, payment will be handled by Google Play or the App Store, we will receive only an entitlement (which plan you hold), and this policy will be updated before that build is submitted.

8. Children

ClearScan collects nothing from anyone, including children, because it collects nothing at all (see sections 2 and 3). There is no account a child could create and no data a parent would need us to delete from a server, because we hold no copy of anything. Everything on the phone is removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

9. Deleting your data

Because we hold no copy, there is nothing to ask us to delete. To remove everything ClearScan stores, clear the app's data or uninstall the app on your phone; that removes the app's private directory, including the library index, the page images, and the app lock file. If you shared a document through the share sheet, the copy you sent lives wherever you sent it, and only that destination can remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

If ClearScan gains a feature that changes any claim above (an account, a cloud feature, a live payment path, analytics), this policy is updated before that build is submitted, the change is named in the update, and the new version is posted at this same address with a new effective date.
