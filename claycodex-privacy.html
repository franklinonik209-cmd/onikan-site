ClayCodex privacy policy

Metropolitan Museum of Art, Open Access. License: CC0.

ClayCodex (Ancient Texts, Explained) is made by Onikan Global Solutions LLC. Contact: contact@onikanglobal.com. This policy describes what the ClayCodex app does with your information.

What we collect

Account information (only if you sign in)

If you create an account or sign in, we collect:

  • Your email address and a user ID assigned by our sign-in provider.
  • Your password is handled by the sign-in provider only. We never see or store your password in readable form.

You can read the library without an account. Account features (saved reading settings, explanation allowances) require sign-in, and the app says so honestly when a build is not configured for accounts.

Reading settings (only if you are signed in)

Three preferences, and nothing else:

  • Text size (standard or large).
  • Whether narration auto-plays (a preference only; see section 2.3).
  • Whether you want a daily reading reminder (a preference only; reminders are not sent yet).

These settings sync across your devices through your account.

Explanation requests

When you ask for an explanation of a passage, the app sends to our server either the passage reference (text, chapter, verse range from our catalog) or text you pasted yourself. Our server returns a plain-English explanation. Today that explanation comes from our in-house demo explainer, which labels its output as a sample explanation while full explanations are being prepared. Passage text is never written to our logs.

We store a per-plan usage count (how many explanations you have used against your plan's daily allowance) so the free allowance can be enforced. We do not store a history of your questions.

What we do NOT collect

  • No bookmarks and no reading position. The app has no bookmark feature and does not track where you stopped reading.
  • No listening activity. No narration files exist yet, playback is local, and no listening event is sent to us.
  • No analytics and no crash reports. The app ships no analytics or crash reporting software.
  • No contacts, no photos, no precise location, no device advertising ID.
  • No payment card numbers and no government IDs. No payment is taken today (see section 4).
  • No children's data is knowingly collected; accounts are not directed at children under 13 (see section 8).

How we use what we collect

  • To operate the service: sign you in, keep your session working, and serve the library and explanations you request.
  • To sync your three reading settings across devices.
  • To enforce plan allowances (the free plan allows up to 5 explanations per day).
  • To prevent abuse of the explanation feature.
  • To communicate with you about your account, if you contact us.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your questions to train our own models. Your data is not used for advertising.

Who receives your data

Named recipients only. No other third party receives your data.

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore), project claycodex. Firebase holds your account (email and user ID) for sign-in and holds your settings and usage counters for sync. Firebase does not receive your questions, your reading history (we do not keep one), or any payment data.
  • Nobody else today. No analytics provider, no crash provider, no AI provider, and no payment provider receives anything from this app in the current build, because none is wired in.

When a future feature adds a recipient, this policy is updated in the same change, before that feature ships. See section 9.

Payments

There are no payments in the current build. Billing is not activated and no store product exists, so no purchase can be made. The subscription screen shows the monthly and yearly subscription plans of record (yearly with a 7-day trial) as plans that open at launch, never as a price you can pay today; lifetime and per-collection offers were retired under the 2026-10-25 subscription doctrine. Before any payment feature ships, this section will be replaced with the exact prices, billing period, auto-renewal terms, trial terms, refund terms, and cancellation path, and the store products will be created first. Until then, any line in older material that names a sell price for this app is superseded and must not be relied on.

Explanations and AI: the honest state today

Explanations today are sample explanations from our demo explainer, clearly labeled in the app as samples, while full explanations are being prepared. No explanation request leaves our server for any outside AI provider today, and no outside party reads your questions.

Data retention and deletion

  • Reading settings and usage counters are kept while your account is active, so sync and allowances keep working.
  • Passage text from explanation requests is not logged and is not retained.
  • Account deletion: in the app, Settings > Delete account deletes your account and the reading settings stored with it, and signs you out; the books stay free to read without an account. The in-app button is implemented in code (roster block 2026-10-29) and takes effect when this policy goes live with the release that carries it; until that release ships, you may ask us to delete your account and its stored settings by emailing contact@onikanglobal.com from the email address on the account; we will confirm and delete within 30 days, subject to any billing records the law requires us to keep (none exist today, since no payment is taken). In-app account deletion is a launch requirement, not an optional extra, because both stores require it for apps with account creation.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. In release builds the app refuses any non-HTTPS server address. Sign-in tokens are kept in the device's secure storage (keystore/keychain), never in plain text. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you as the law requires.

Children

The service is not directed at children under 13, and under-13 accounts are not permitted. If we discover an account held by a child under 13, we will delete it. Paid accounts, when payments exist, will require the account holder to be 18 or older or to buy through a parent-managed store purchase path.

Changes to this policy and contact

We will post material changes here before they take effect, with a new effective date. Questions or deletion requests: contact@onikanglobal.com.
