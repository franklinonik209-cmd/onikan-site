Frostreak privacy policy

Metropolitan Museum of Art, Open Access. License: CC0.

Frostreak is made by Onikan Global Solutions LLC. This policy describes what the Frostreak app does with your information. The short version: Frostreak collects nothing.

Effective October 25, 2026.

What Frostreak collects

Nothing. Frostreak has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers. The app contains no network code, and it does not send your information anywhere.

Where your data lives

Your habits, check-ins, and settings are stored only on your device, in an encrypted store whose key is kept in your phone’s secure storage. Frostreak data is excluded from your phone’s automatic cloud backup of the app.

What leaves your device

Only what you send yourself:

  • A poster image, when you choose to save or share one through your phone’s share sheet.
  • A backup file (your habits, check-ins, and settings in a readable JSON file), when you choose to export one. The backup file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe. Restoring a backup replaces the data in the app only after you confirm, and a file that does not validate is rejected without changing your data. Restoring a backup never resets the app’s one free poster export: if you have already used it on this device, it stays used.

Purchases

Frostreak offers subscriptions and a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google processes those payments; Frostreak does not receive or store your payment details.

Deleting your data

You can delete habits inside the app, erase the app’s data from its recovery screen, or uninstall the app, which removes all of its data from your device. Because nothing is collected, there is no copy on any server to delete.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this page before it takes effect. Questions: the developer contact shown on Frostreak’s Google Play listing.
