Tablets of Destiny privacy policy

Metropolitan Museum of Art, Open Access. License: CC0.

Tablets of Destiny is made by Onikan Global Solutions LLC. Contact: contact@onikanglobal.com. This policy describes what the Tablets of Destiny app does with your information.

Effective November 2, 2026.

Short version

Tablets of Destiny collects no personal data, shares no personal data, and has no account, no server, and no network connection of its own. The game is fully offline. Your reigns, your chronicle, and your settings stay on your device. There are no ads, no analytics, and no crash reporting in the app.

What we do not collect

We do not collect, and the app does not transmit, any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact details. There is no sign-in, so there is no account to attach them to.
  • Your location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or device identifiers. The app asks for none of these.
  • Your advertising ID. There is no advertising SDK in the app, so there is nothing that reads it.
  • Payment card numbers or any other payment data. No purchase can be completed today (see the Purchases section), and when subscriptions are activated, payment is handled by the store (Google Play or the App Store), not by this app.
  • Anything about how you play beyond what the next section stores locally on your device. No play data is sent anywhere for analytics, advertising, or any other purpose.

What stays on your device

The following live only in the app's local storage on your device. Nothing in this list is uploaded, synced, or shared by the app itself:

  • Your saved reigns: the state of the world, your choices, and the chronicle the game writes for each reign.
  • Your settings and preferences.
  • Your progress records, including unlocked codex entries and achievements tracked by the game.

The game holds no social accounts, embeds no social SDK, and posts nothing on its own. Audio and art in the game are bundled with the app and played from those bundled files. Nothing is streamed.

No advertising, no analytics, no tracking

Tablets of Destiny contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no crash-reporting SDK. There are no ads in any tier. Nothing in the app tracks you across apps or websites, and nothing is used for advertising. Adding an advertising SDK in the future would require a new product decision and a rewritten privacy posture, because an ad SDK collects device data by design.

Purchases

No purchase can be completed today. The subscription products of record (tablets_pro_monthly and tablets_pro_yearly) exist in the store in draft, inactive form, behind a dark seam in the app, and the store is never called while the seam is dark. Era 1 of the game is complete and free.

When subscriptions are activated, purchases will be handled by Google Play or the App Store. The app will receive an entitlement (which plan you hold) and will never receive your payment data. At activation, that path is re-checked before any purchase proof is claimed.

Children

Because the app collects nothing and transmits nothing, it collects no personal data from anyone, including children. There is no account, no chat, no messaging, and no user content sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

There is no server copy to delete, because there is no server. Deleting the app deletes its local storage with it, including your saved reigns, chronicle, settings, and progress records.

If you have a question about this policy, contact contact@onikanglobal.com. There is no account to delete and no developer-held data to return, so questions are answered on that basis.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, the new version will be posted at the same public address before it takes effect, with a new effective date. A change that starts collecting or sharing data would be a new product decision, not a quiet edit: it would require a rewritten data safety record as well as this policy.
