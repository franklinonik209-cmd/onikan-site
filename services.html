How the hosting works, in plain words

Your site is hosted on a major provider's free tier for static websites, the same kind of setup behind our own site at onikanglobal.com. There is no monthly hosting bill. Free tiers have published limits on size and traffic that a five-page site for a local business or an author will not come near. If your site ever outgrows the free tier, paid hosting starts at a few dollars a month and the site moves with you, because everything is in your name. This package covers brochure and author websites. It does not include online stores or payment processing; if you need to sell online, we will tell you plainly which store platform fits before you spend anything.

Email, in plain words

The free email plan includes up to 5 mailboxes with 5 GB of storage each, used through a web browser. If you want email inside Outlook or a phone mail app, or more storage, paid plans exist (Zoho Mail Lite, or Google Workspace at $7 per user per month). You pay the provider directly if you choose one. We will show you both options with the real prices before you decide.
