ScoreLedger privacy policy

Metropolitan Museum of Art, Open Access. License: CC0.

ScoreLedger is published by Onikan Global Solutions LLC. Contact: contact@onikanglobal.com. This policy describes what the ScoreLedger app does with information.

Effective November 16, 2026.

1. The short version

The app collects nothing. ScoreLedger has no account, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. Everything the app stores lives on your device, in an encrypted local store, and none of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else. We have no server that receives it.

2. What the app stores, and where

The app stores the following on your device only, in an encrypted local store protected by a key held in your device's keystore (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore):

  • Your settings, including appearance and region.
  • Profiles you create: a name and the ingredients and allergens you state that you avoid.
  • Your scan and view history, with a copy of the product record you saw.
  • Lists and menus you save.
  • Label captures and mismatch reports you choose to save.

None of this is transmitted to us or to anyone else.

3. Network use

This build of the app makes no network calls. Product data ships with the app and in data packs you choose to install. Scores are computed on your device.

4. Sharing a report

You can share a product report as a link. The report (the score, its arithmetic, its data source, and the formula version) travels inside the link itself, in the part of the link that browsers do not send to servers. Whoever you send the link to can open it in a browser and view the report until the expiry you chose. The link contains no profile data, no history, and nothing about you. A link cannot be cancelled early; it stops working when it expires.

5. Your controls

  • Export: Settings, Your data, Export my data produces a copy of everything the app stores on your device, as a JSON document.
  • Delete: Settings, Your data, Delete all my data erases everything the app stores on your device. Because your data exists only on your device, deletion is complete.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its store and its key.

6. Children

The app is a general-audience information tool. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes

If a future version adds any feature that moves data off the device (for example, optional sync), this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the feature will be opt-in.
