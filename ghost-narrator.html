Ghost Narrator: your audiobook, produced for you, owned by you.

A studio narrator charges $2,000 to $5,000 to turn a finished book into a finished audiobook. Most indie authors never pay it, so their books earn in print and earn nothing in audio.

Ghost Narrator is the done-for-you alternative from Onikan Global Solutions. Send us your manuscript. We narrate it, check every name and term with you, master it to audiobook standard, and deliver finished files that are yours outright. Not rented. Not locked to one store. Yours.

Half the market price. $200 per finished hour plus a $300 production fee, $1,300 minimum, $2,800 maximum, half up front and half on delivery. A typical seven-hour book costs $1,700, against $2,000 to $5,000 the traditional way.

Your voice, if you want it. Choose a professional studio voice, or narrate in your own cloned voice under a recorded consent session, built for your book only and deleted after delivery.

Straight answers about stores. Spotify and direct sales welcome disclosed digital narration, and we will show you exactly how to place your book there. ACX bars AI narration, and we will never pretend otherwise. Every master we deliver carries its digital-narration disclosure in the open.

How it works. Tell us about your book. Get a fixed quote in writing. Send the manuscript. Approve a sample chapter in your chosen voice. Review your chapters. Receive your masters and a plain-English guide to putting them on sale.

Ghost Narrator is a production service of Onikan Global Solutions LLC. Write to us at contact@onikanglobal.com and tell us about your book.
