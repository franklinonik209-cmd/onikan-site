Resolution Desk

For law firms

Your staff loses hours on hold. We make the calls.

The Resolution Desk is an outbound calling service for solo and small law firms. You hand us a call to make: an insurer who has not answered, a records department sitting on a file, a clerk’s office, a client who needs confirming. We work the hold music and the phone menu, inside limits you set in writing, and you receive the full transcript, the outcome, and the next step.

$39 per resolved task. Never per minute. A task that ends with no resolution and no named next step is not billed. Your first resolved task is free, so you judge the transcript, not a pitch.

The work

Four kinds of calls, one flat price per result

Every engagement is a short written brief: who to call, the objective, the facts the caller may use, and hard limits on what may be confirmed, offered, or disclosed. The caller never works outside the brief.

Insurance claim status

Carrier claim lines and adjuster desks. We chase the status, the missing document, the payment that was promised, and we bring back a named answer: the status, the missing item, or the dated commitment, quoted from the call.

Records chases

Medical providers, prior counsel, employers, and agencies holding records your file needs. We confirm what was received, what is still missing, and exactly what clears the release, so your next step is a fact, not a guess.

Court clerk and agency follow-ups

Filing status, hearing logistics, document requests, agency case status. We gather and confirm only. We never argue a position and never give legal advice, to anyone, on any call.

Scheduling and confirmations

Client appointment confirmations, witness and vendor scheduling, reschedules inside windows you set. You get a confirmed time, or a reached party with a named callback time.

How it works

  1. 1

    You send the brief: the number, the objective, the facts, and the limits. Five minutes of writing replaces an hour of your staff’s day.

  2. 2

    We make the call and work it: the menu, the hold, the transfer chain, the callback when a line goes nowhere. Retries inside the same task cost you nothing extra.

  3. 3

    You receive the full transcript, the structured outcome, and the next step with a name attached to it. The record belongs to your file.

  4. 4

    If the call reaches the edge of your limits, we stop and escalate to you with the transcript so far. Nothing is ever improvised in your firm’s name.

Trust

The rules we work by

Every call discloses itself. Our caller opens by stating it is an AI assistant calling on behalf of your firm. That is our standard, on every call, without exception. Your firm’s name is never used behind a pretense.

We gather and chase. We never advise. The desk does not give legal advice, does not argue, and does not hold itself out as counsel or as your staff. It confirms facts, chases answers, and schedules.

Your limits are hard limits. What the brief says may never be disclosed is never disclosed. What the brief does not authorize is not offered, confirmed, or negotiated. The edge of the brief is the edge of the call.

Not resolved, not billed. A task bills when the objective is reached, or when you hold a definitive answer with a named next step. Dead ends are worked and reported, and they cost you nothing.
