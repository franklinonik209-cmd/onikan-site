Heritage Audio

The Yoruba of home, carried in stories.

A Yoruba audio program for the diaspora, built the way the language was always passed down: through stories, proverbs, and the scenes of everyday family life. For your ears, your drive, your pace. And for the child you want to hear it after you.

You understand more Yoruba than you can say. You catch your mother's meaning before she finishes the sentence, and then the reply will not come. Or you speak it well enough at home and watch it thin out in your children, one school year at a time.

Heritage Audio is being made for that exact place. It is not a classroom and it is not an app full of drills. It is a program of stories, each one carrying the words, the proverbs, and the rhythms of Yoruba as it is spoken in family life, with a workbook that walks beside the audio so the language settles and stays.

We are making it as a keepsake as much as a program: something a parent can hand to a child, something you can give to the person in your family who keeps saying, one day I will learn.

What is inside

  • A complete audio program of Yoruba stories, proverbs, and everyday scenes, made audio-first so it fits the commute, the kitchen, and the quiet hour.
  • Narration in the voice of the program's creator, Franklin Onikan, a native Yoruba speaker born in Nigeria (see the honest note about the voice below).
  • A companion workbook that follows the stories, so you can see the words, sit with the proverbs, and mark how far you have come.
  • Yours outright. One payment, no subscription, delivered digitally to your email when the program ships.

The honest note about the voice

The narration is produced with a clone of Franklin Onikan's own voice, recorded and made by him, and used with his consent. We tell you this plainly because a heritage program should be honest about how it was made. The voice is his. The stories are ours. The decision to use the clone is what lets one native voice carry a whole program to families everywhere at a price a family can pay.
