The setup

What the setup includes

Every item below is switched on, set correctly, and verified on your machine, then recorded in your written report.

On a Windows computer

  • Windows brought fully up to date, with automatic updates set to stay on
  • Microsoft Defender fully armed: real-time protection, cloud-delivered protection, and tamper protection on
  • Ransomware folder guard on, so your documents are protected against encryption attacks
  • SmartScreen filtering on, to warn you away from dangerous apps, files, and websites
  • Firewall on for every kind of network
  • BitLocker disk encryption on. The recovery key goes to your own Microsoft account or is printed for you. We never hold it.
  • Scam blocking at the network level: known scam, phishing, and malware sites are blocked before they load
  • Browser hardened: an ad and pop-up blocker installed (this is what stops the fake virus warning pop-ups), secure connections enforced, pop-ups blocked
  • Your everyday account set to standard, with a separate password-protected admin account, so nothing can install itself silently
  • Startup programs reviewed. Leftover remote-access tools are flagged and removed unless you use them on purpose; that leftover access is the door most tech support scams walk through
  • Your backup checked and confirmed working
  • A guided safety check of your main email account, with two-step verification confirmed. You type everything yourself
  • Public Wi-Fi safety set: your computer will not join open networks on its own, and secure name lookups are on

On a Mac

  • macOS updated to the newest version your machine supports, with automatic updates on
  • FileVault disk encryption on. The recovery key goes to your iCloud or is printed for you. We never hold it.
  • Gatekeeper verified and XProtect confirmed active: the Mac’s own built-in malware protection
  • Firewall on, with stealth mode on
  • A free outbound connection watcher installed, and a plain-words lesson in what its prompts mean
  • Scam blocking at the network level, the same as on Windows: known scam, phishing, and malware sites blocked before they load
  • Browser hardened: a content blocker in Safari or a blocker in Chrome or Firefox, secure connections on, pop-ups blocked
  • Your everyday account set to standard, with a separate admin account and a strong password; Touch ID set up where your machine has it
  • Time Machine backup verified running, or set up on your drive
  • A guided safety check of your main email account, with two-step verification confirmed. You type everything yourself
  • Public Wi-Fi safety set: your computer will not join open networks on its own, and secure name lookups are on

Trust

The rules we work by

You type every password yourself. We never ask for, receive, or store your passwords. We never hold your encryption recovery key either; it goes to your own account or is printed for you.

Your computer’s own protection, fully armed. We are not an antivirus company, and this service is not our own antivirus software. The malware protection is the platform’s own, Microsoft Defender on Windows and XProtect and Gatekeeper on a Mac, switched on, set correctly, and verified by us.

No promises no one can keep. No setup can promise that a computer will never be infected, and we will not pretend otherwise. What we promise is that every protection on the checklist is on, set correctly, and verified, and that you hold the written proof.

Already infected? The setup stops. Cleanup is a different job. It is quoted separately and plainly before any work begins. The setup price is not a cleanup price.
