NaijaTax Ledger privacy policy

Metropolitan Museum of Art, Open Access. License: CC0.

NaijaTax Ledger is made by Onikan Global Solutions LLC. This policy describes what the NaijaTax Ledger app does with your information. The short version: the app collects nothing, and your tax records never leave your phone.

Effective date: October 22, 2026.

What NaijaTax Ledger collects

Nothing. NaijaTax Ledger has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no servers receiving your data. The app holds no network permission, so it cannot send your information anywhere even if it wanted to.

Where your data lives

Your income and expense entries, your settings, and your receipt photos are stored only on your device, in a single encrypted store (AES-256 encryption) whose key is kept in your phone’s secure storage. Receipt photos are encrypted on the device as well and can be viewed only inside the app. NaijaTax Ledger data is excluded from your phone’s automatic cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer of the app’s data.

What the app never asks for

NaijaTax Ledger never asks for, accepts, or stores a National Identification Number (NIN), a Tax ID number, a Bank Verification Number (BVN), or any other government ID number. The app includes an educational explanation of how Tax ID works in Nigeria; the explanation needs no number from you, and the confirmation links in it open official pages in your own browser.

What leaves your device

Only what you send yourself:

  • An export of your records, or your state filing pack, when you choose to create one and share it through your phone’s share sheet. Nothing is exported or shared automatically.
  • Links to official pages, when you tap one. They open in your system browser, outside the app; your browsing there is between you and that site.

Deadline reminders are generated on your device. There are no push notifications from a server, because there is no server.

Purchases

NaijaTax Ledger is free to download and offers a one-time unlock through Google Play. Google processes that payment; NaijaTax Ledger does not receive or store your payment details.

Deleting your data

Use the “Delete all my data” action inside the app to delete your records, your receipt photos, and the encryption key. You can also clear the app’s data in your phone settings, or uninstall the app, which removes all of its data from your device. Because nothing is collected, there is no copy on any server to delete.

Who the app is for

NaijaTax Ledger is a tax record keeper for adults handling their own Nigerian personal income tax. It is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this page before it takes effect. Questions: contact@onikanglobal.com, or the developer contact shown on NaijaTax Ledger’s Google Play listing.
